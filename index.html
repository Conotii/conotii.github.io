Studio

A private, self-hosted dashboard for managing my own YouTube channels.

What Studio does

  • Shows analytics of my own YouTube channels (views, watch time, subscribers, impressions, click-through rate, estimated revenue) in one place.
  • Helps me plan, edit and prepare short-form videos and thumbnails before I upload them myself.
  • Runs on my own server. There is no public sign-up; only the owner of the channels uses it.

Google account access

Studio asks for read-only access to YouTube data and YouTube Analytics of the channels I connect. It does not upload, edit or delete videos, and it does not access any other Google data. See the privacy policy for details.
